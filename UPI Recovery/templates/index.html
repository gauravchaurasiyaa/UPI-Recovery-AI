How It Works

A simple process to understand your UPI problem and prepare for the appropriate recovery steps.

01. Report Your Problem

Enter your UPI transaction details, select the type of payment problem and explain what happened.

02. AI Transaction Analysis

Our AI assistant analyses your description and identifies the possible issue, such as a wrong transfer, suspected fraud, duplicate payment or pending transaction.

03. Get Recovery Guidance

Receive personalised recommendations, an evidence checklist and practical precautions based on your reported problem.

04. Prepare Your Complaint

Generate a professional complaint draft containing your transaction details. You can review it before sending it to your bank.

05. Track Your Case

View your saved recovery cases, check their locally recorded status and access your generated complaint.

06. Stay Financially Safe

Learn important precautions against UPI scams, suspicious payment requests, OTP fraud and fake recovery services.
